Are the regular and no onion-garlic meals cooked separately?

Yes — completely separately. Our no onion-garlic Jain/Satvik meals are prepared in entirely dedicated vessels, on separate cooking stations. The regular onion-garlic meals and the no onion-garlic meals never share the same cookware, utensils, or cooking area. This is our firm commitment to customers following Jain and Satvik diets.

What is Satvik food?

Satvik food is a pure vegetarian diet from Indian Ayurvedic tradition. It excludes onion, garlic, and certain root vegetables (such as potatoes, carrots, and turnips in strict Jain interpretation). Satvik food is considered clean, light, and nourishing for both physical health and mental clarity. SatvikMeals specialises in authentic Satvik and Jain cooking.
