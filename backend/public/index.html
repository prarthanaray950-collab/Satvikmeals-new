About SatvikMeals

A meal service built on
discipline & trust

SatvikMeals is Patna's homegrown 100% pure vegetarian tiffin service, committed to delivering fresh, hygienic, home-style meals to students, professionals, and families across the city.

Every meal is prepared with the same care you would expect in your own home. We never compromise on hygiene, ingredient quality, or the consistency of taste — ever. Our kitchen follows strict sanitation protocols and every meal is taste-tested before dispatch.

From a small kitchen serving a handful of families, we've grown to over 200 happy subscribers while maintaining the same standards that made our first customers trust us.
