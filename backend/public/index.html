FAQ

Common questions answered.

Does SatvikMeals deliver instantly or only via subscription?

SatvikMeals provides both immediate on-demand food delivery and daily tiffin subscription plans across Patna. You do not need a subscription to order — you can get a single meal delivered right away by WhatsApp or calling us.

Do you offer no onion no garlic meals?

Yes. We offer both regular vegetarian meals and no onion-garlic Jain/Satvik meals. Critically, both variants are cooked completely separately in dedicated vessels — there is no cross-contamination whatsoever, making them safe for the strictest Jain and Satvik dietary requirements.

Does SatvikMeals offer Jain food delivery in Patna?

Yes. SatvikMeals is one of the only dedicated Jain food delivery services in Patna. Our Jain meals are prepared without onion, garlic, or root vegetables, in separate vessels, following Jain dietary principles strictly.

Is SatvikMeals 100% pure vegetarian?

Yes. Our kitchen is strictly 100% pure vegetarian. No eggs, no meat, no non-veg ingredients of any kind are used or stored in our kitchen at any time.

Which areas of Patna do you deliver to?

We deliver across Patna including Boring Road, Bailey Road, Anandpuri, Rajendra Nagar, Kankarbagh, Gardanibagh, Patliputra Colony, Ashok Nagar, Kidwaipuri, Shastri Nagar, Ramkripal Nagar, and Jaganpura. Not sure if we cover your area? WhatsApp us.

Can I order a single meal without a subscription?

Yes. You can order an individual meal for immediate delivery without any subscription plan. Just WhatsApp or call us to place your order.

How do I confirm a plan or place an order?

Simply WhatsApp us at +91 90314 47621 or call +91 62012 76506. You can also sign up on our website, select a plan, and tap "WhatsApp Us" — your plan details will be pre-filled in the message automatically.
