Patna's Only Dedicated Jain Tiffin Service

For the Jain community in Patna, finding truly compliant food delivery has always been difficult. Most services offer generic vegetarian food and simply omit onion and garlic on request — but use the same vessels, the same oil, and the same utensils. That is not Jain food. That is cross-contaminated food.

SatvikMeals is built differently. We operate a dedicated Jain cooking line that is physically and procedurally separate from our regular vegetarian cooking. Our Jain meals are prepared by cooks who understand and respect Jain dietary principles — not as a special request, but as a standard operating procedure.

Our Jain Promise: No onion, no garlic, no root vegetables — cooked in completely separate vessels, with separate utensils, on separate cooking stations. Zero cross-contamination. Every single day.

What Jain Dietary Rules Do We Follow?

✅
Pure Vegetarian
100% veg kitchen. No meat, no eggs, no non-veg ingredients of any kind.
✅
Separate Cooking
Jain meals prepared in separate vessels, utensils, and on dedicated cooking stations.

No Onion
Onion excluded from all Jain meal preparations — not even as a base or garnish.

Garlic excluded entirely — including garlic powder, garlic oil, or any garlic derivative.

Fresh Daily
Cooked fresh every day. No overnight storage of prepared food. No preservatives.
