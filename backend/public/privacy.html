Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

SatvikMeals ("we", "us", "our"), based in Patna, Bihar, runs a pure-vegetarian tiffin and food-delivery service. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use satvikmeals.in, why we collect it, and how we look after it. We only collect what we need to cook and deliver your food and to run your account.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details — your name and email address, provided when you sign in with Google.
  • Contact number — your mobile number, so we can confirm orders and coordinate delivery over call or WhatsApp.
  • Delivery location — the address and, if you allow it, the GPS coordinates you share so our delivery reaches the right door.
  • Order & subscription history — the plans and meals you order, and your dietary preferences (e.g. Jain, no onion-garlic).
  • Messages you send us — complaints, suggestions and support requests.

2. How we use your information

  • To prepare and deliver your meals to the correct address.
  • To manage your tiffin subscription — activation, pausing, renewal and expiry reminders.
  • To contact you about your orders by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To respond to your questions, complaints and feedback.
  • To keep our service secure and prevent misuse.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the services that make our website and communication work:

  • Google Sign-In — to let you log in securely.
  • WhatsApp — to send you order and account updates.
  • Our email provider — to send you transactional emails (welcome, plan activation, reminders).
  • Our delivery staff, who receive only the details needed to bring your food.

We may disclose information if required by law.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is active, and afterwards only as long as needed for legitimate business or legal reasons. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

5. Your choices

  • You can update your phone number and delivery location from your dashboard.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your personal information by messaging us.
  • You can opt out of non-essential messages while still receiving essential order updates.

6. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies that are necessary for the site to work — for example, a security token that protects your account from cross-site request forgery. We do not use advertising cookies.
